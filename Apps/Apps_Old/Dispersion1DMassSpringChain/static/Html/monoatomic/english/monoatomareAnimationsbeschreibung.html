Animation of the Monoatomic Mass-Spring Chain Normal Modes

In the following, the normal modes of a monoatomic mass-spring chain can be visualized. For this purpose, a segment of the structure consisting of $N+1$ masses has been linked using the periodic boundary conditions of the Bloch theorem. The immutable parameters of the animation are:

  • Mass $m = 1$
  • Spring constant $c = 1$
  • Distance between unit cells $a = 4$
  • Number of visualized masses $N = 32$ (or number of normal modes)
To visualize the normal modes, the real part of $u_n(t)=A(k(\omega))\text{e}^{\text{i}(\mu n - \omega t)}$ is calculated.
Thus, the motion of mass $m_n$ is proportional to $\cos(\mu n-\omega t)$.
Using the & buttons, the values of the dimensionless wave vector $\mu$ within the first Brillouin zone $[-\pi,\pi]$ can be selected, and the corresponding normal modes can be visualized in the subsequent depiction of the mass-spring chain.
The button can be used to visualize the frequency band gaps.
With the button, the animation can be started and stopped.

Special Normal Modes:
At the boundaries of the reduced Brillouin zone $[0;\pi]$, the two extremes can be observed. For $\mu =+ \pi$, it holds: $ \frac{\mathrm{d} \omega}{\mathrm{d} k} = 0$ and standing waves occur.
In this case, no energy transfer takes place, and the masses oscillate in opposite phases.
