Observation:

For low mean values of $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$, $\frac{\omega}{k}=\frac{\mathrm{d}\omega}{\mathrm{d} k}$ holds, and the system behaves non-dispersive.
As the mean values of $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ increase, the group velocity decreases more rapidly than the phase velocity. The system behaves dispersive. The carrier wave moves through the envelope of the wave packets.
