Dispersion Properties of the monoatomic Mass-Spring-Chain

This application visualizes the dispersion properties of the monoatomic mass-spring chain. The first section of the application summarizes the derivation of the dispersion relation. Following this, the dispersion ratio $\omega(k)$ is depicted, allowing for the representation of the normal modes of the chain through interactive selection of dimensionless wave vectors. In the subsequent representation, two modes can be visualized to illustrate the velocity differences between phases and group velocities. Additionally, the superposition of two normal modes can be visualized.

Structure of the One-Dimensional Monoatomic Mass-Spring Chain

The system of the monoatomic mass-spring chain consists of an infinite number of periodically arranged, equally sized masses $m$. Here, adjacent masses are connected by a linear-elastic spring with spring stiffness $c$. The distance between the initial positions of the masses corresponds to $a$. Due to the periodicity of the system, it can be simplified by describing it over the unit cell. The unit cell contains the smallest periodically repeating structure, consisting of a mass and the springs connected to it.
