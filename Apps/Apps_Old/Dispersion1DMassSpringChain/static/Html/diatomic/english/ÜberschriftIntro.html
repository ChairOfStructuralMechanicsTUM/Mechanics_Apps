Dispersion Properties of Diatomic Mass-Spring Chain

This app visualizes the dispersion properties of the diatomic mass-spring chain. In the first section of the app, the derivation of the dispersion relation is summarized. In the subsequent section, the dispersion ratio $\omega(\mu)$ is depicted, and the normal modes of the chain can be represented by an interactive selection of dimensionless wave vectors $\mu$. With the last visualization, two modes of the two frequency branches can be visualized to illustrate the differences in phase velocity and group velocity.

Structure of the One-Dimensional Diatomic Mass-Spring Chain

The system of the diatomic mass-spring chain consists of the periodic sequence of two different masses $m_1$ and $m_2$. Hereinafter, it holds that:

$m_1 \geq m_2$.

Adjacent masses are connected by a linear-elastic spring with spring constant $c$. The distance between the initial positions of the masses corresponds to $\frac{a}{2}$. Due to the periodicity of the system, it can be simplified by describing it over the unit cell. The unit cell contains the smallest periodically repeating structure, consisting of the masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ and the springs connected to them.
