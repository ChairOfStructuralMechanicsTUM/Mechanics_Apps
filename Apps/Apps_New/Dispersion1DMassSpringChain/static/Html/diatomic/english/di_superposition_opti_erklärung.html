Observations:

For $$\mu=0$$, $$\frac{\omega}{k}\neq 0$$ and $$\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial k}=0$$.
With increasing mean values of $$\mu_1$$ and $$\mu_2$$, the group velocity $$\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial k}<0$$, while the phase velocity $$\frac{\omega}{k}>0$$. Thus, the carrier wave moves through the envelope of the wave packets in the positive x-direction, while the envelope moves in the negative x-direction. For $$\mu=\pi$$, $$\frac{\omega}{k}\neq 0$$ and $$\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial k}=0$$. The system behaves dispersive.
