Observation:

For low mean values of $$\mu_1$$ and $$\mu_2$$, $$\frac{\omega}{k}=\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial k}$$ holds, and the system behaves non-dispersive.
With increasing mean values of $$\mu_1$$ and $$\mu_2$$, the group velocity decreases more strongly than the phase velocity. The system behaves dispersive. The carrier wave moves through the envelope of the wave packets.

Superposition of Two Optical Normal Modes
